A Family Getaway to Phuket

1–5 October 2026 · 4 nights · 2 adults and 2 children

At a glance

We've listed four options that are located on Phuket itself, two of which are villas, while the fifth is an Anantara on Koh Yao Yai as an additional option for you to consider. You can think of Koh Yao Yai as the “Maldives” of this area. It is very quiet and probably the most unspoiled island in the bay. However, it does require an airport transfer to the speedboat jetty, followed by a speedboat ride, which is complimentary as part of the benefits for this rate. It is a bit of a transfer, but worthwhile if you would like a quiet beach getaway that's more off the beaten path.

Another thing to note is that October is traditionally a fairly rainy season in Phuket, which is reflected in some of the lower rates you will see at the hotels. With that in mind, I've also kept facilities and activities within the resort or room itself in the back of my head, in case you do encounter some rainy days. That said, one of our colleagues is based in Phuket and I did ask her about the weather. She mentioned that it really depends on the year: the year before had lots of rain, whereas last year was perfectly sunny.

Where they are

The airport sits north of the island, and amongst the Phuket options The Slate is the closest at ten minutes away and the InterContinental the furthest at forty. Everything on Phuket faces west into the Andaman Sea. Koh Yao Yai lies behind the island in Phang Nga Bay, sheltered by it, with a quieter vibe.

Worth knowing

This is a unique, quirky hotel, and you'll see this reflected in the room and hotel design, which has a tin mining theme. The hotel is family-owned and run by a third-generation tin mining family in Phuket, offering a definitely different experience compared to a larger corporate resort.

Note that it's also very family friendly. There is a good kids' club, a babysitting service is available, and there is not just a family pool but also a shallow infant pool next to it.

Choosing Between Them

For a boutique hotel feel, I'd suggest going with The Slate. For a large, classic luxury resort experience I highly recommend the InterContinental.

If you're looking for a villa option on Phuket, I think both Banyan Tree and Anantara Mai Khao are solid options. The Anantara Mai Khao rooms feel fresher and I feel this is reflected in the price.

That said, if you don't mind a boat transfer (which I think is worthwhile: there is a nice lounge at the boat terminal with a kids area, and the speedboat is quite luxurious and an experience in itself), I rate Anantara Koh Yao Yai very highly. It's the newest property, opened in August 2023, and I think they are very thoughtful about creating a child-centric experience, from the speedboat transfer through to the stay. For instance, their Family Suite has a curtain around the bunk beds, and their Lagoon Pool Villa is my favourite layout of all the villas. It actually has a proper separate living room, unlike the other two villa options on Phuket.
