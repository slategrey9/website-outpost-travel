The three

South Ari Atoll

Conrad Maldives Rangali Island

Two Bedroom Deluxe Beach Villa with Pool · 300 sqm

USD 9,613
Total for four

The villa

One king and two twins across 300 square metres, the largest of the three. The second bedroom sits well back down the garden with its own bathroom, which makes it the most private arrangement here. There is no separate living area, so the room facing the pool is the main bedroom.

Why this one

Probably the most iconic resort in the Maldives, spanning two natural islands connected by a footbridge. Whale sharks and manta rays are sighted here year round, and Ithaa, the underwater restaurant, is on this island.

What is included

  • Half board, breakfast and dinner daily
  • Shared return seaplane transfers
  • Early check-in and late check-out, subject to availability
  • 25 per cent off treatments at the spa
conradmaldives.com

Getting there

Seaplane · 30 minutes

Complimentary private lounge access at the seaplane terminal on arrival.

Worth knowing

From 1 July to 14 September 2026, The Spa Retreat, Mandhoo, Grand Water Villas and Muraka will undergo enhancements. Guest experience remains unchanged, with wellness services continuing at the Overwater Spa and curated dining available in different venues.

If you take a look at the resort map, you will see the works are in a section set away from the main area, so they should not affect your stay.

Lhaviyani Atoll

Six Senses Kanuhura

Two Bedroom Beach Villa Suite with Pool

USD 11,887
Total for four

The villa

Two bedrooms sit either side of a spacious living room, with a second smaller sitting room off the main bedroom and a proper dining area for meals in the villa. This is the best layout of the three by some distance, and the living room works as a third sleeping space if your daughter would rather not share with you.

Why this one

The beach at Kanuhura is among the finest in the Maldives, powdery and unusually wide. It is the reason people choose this island over its neighbours.

What is included

  • Half board, with dinner across four of the resort restaurants
  • Shared return seaplane transfers
  • USD 100 resort credit
  • Room upgrade on arrival, subject to availability
  • Priority early check-in and late check-out
  • Welcome amenity, a handwritten note, and VIP status on your profile
sixsenses.com/kanuhura

Getting there

Seaplane · 35 minutes

Your daughter travels free on the seaplane.

Raa Atoll

InterContinental Maldives Maamunagau

Family Beach Pool Villa

USD 9,190
Total for four

The villa

A king bedroom with its own bathroom and, set slightly apart, a second room with a sofa bed and a bathroom of its own. A private pool and garden open onto the sand, and the Planet Trekkers kids club is a short walk away.

Why this one

The benefits here are generous. Club InterContinental gives you the lounge throughout the day, including alcohol, alongside daily breakfast, afternoon tea and cakes, and an evening aperitif hour.

What is included

  • Half board, plus Club InterContinental lounge access including alcohol
  • Shared return seaplane transfers, with a private van to the terminal
  • A floating breakfast in your pool, once during the stay
  • Butler service and a 24-hour e-butler
  • A 30-minute photography session with one printed picture
  • Twice-daily housekeeping with evening turndown
maldives.intercontinental.com

Getting there

Seaplane · 35 minutes

A private van collects you at the airport and takes you to a private lounge at the seaplane terminal.

Worth knowing

This is the only one of the three where the second room is a sofa bed rather than a full second bedroom. It is a proper enclosed room with its own bathroom, but if you would rather your helper had a real bed, the other two both offer one.

Free cancellation until 15 days before arrival.

What the prices cover

Included

  • Four nights in the villa described, for all four of you
  • Half board, meaning breakfast and dinner daily
  • Shared return airport transfers by seaplane as noted
  • All government taxes, green tax and service charges
  • The benefits listed under each resort

Exclusions

  • Flights to and from Malé
  • Travel insurance
  • Lunch, alcohol, and anything beyond the half board plan
  • Spa treatments, excursions and diving
  • Gratuities

Seaplanes fly during daylight hours only. This applies to all three resorts. An arrival into Malé after roughly 3.30pm would mean a night in Malé and a transfer the following morning, so it is worth confirming your flight timings early.

All three have a private lounge at the seaplane terminal. Access is generally complimentary on arrival only.

Nothing is held at this stage and availability moves quickly for these dates.
