Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1 · Wednesday 7 October
Arrival, Blue Lagoon & Reykjavík

You land at Keflavík at 10:00am. Soak at Iceland's best known geothermal lagoon on the way from the airport into the city, an easy way to shake off the long-haul flight. Entry tickets and transfers are your own arrangement, matched to your 10:00am landing.

In Reykjavík, the Blue Lagoon shuttle stops at Bus Stop #6, Safnahúsið (The Culture House). From there, either walk 500m (8 min) to Sand Hotel, or take bus 12, 6 or 14 from Lækjartorg B for two stops (2 min) and walk 190m. Buses accept contactless payment (ISK 690 per ride, about SGD 7).

Sand Hotel is a boutique hotel on Laugavegur, Reykjavík's main street, from the team behind Sandholt Bakery next door, a well-known, family-run shop which is one of Reykjavík's most iconic bakery stops. Everything in the centre is within walking distance. The rest of the evening is free for you to decompress, walk the main street and feel the rhythm of the city.

Day 2 · Thursday 8 October
Golden Circle & Midgard Base Camp

Your Midgard guide collects you from the Sand Hotel lobby at 9:00am with your bags. This marks the start of your 4-Day Northern Lights Adventure, a small group tour of up to 16.

The day covers the Golden Circle: Þingvellir National Park, where you walk Almannagjá, the canyon between the North American and Eurasian tectonic plates; the Geysir geothermal area, where Strokkur erupts every few minutes; and Gullfoss, Iceland's most famous waterfall. Lunch is at Friðheimar, a farm known for tomato dishes grown year-round in geothermal greenhouses, and there is a stop at a geothermal bakery, where bread is baked in the warm ground and dug up to taste.

You arrive at Midgard Base Camp in Hvolsvöllur, your home for three nights, for a short briefing and dinner at Midgard Restaurant. The team scouts for the Northern Lights every night and will keep you updated on the forecast. One guided Northern Lights evening tour is included, depending on conditions, and the rooftop hot tub and sauna are there while you wait.

Day 3 · Friday 9 October
Katla Ice Caves & Reynisfjara

After breakfast, you head towards Vík for the Katla Ice Caves, a rare glimpse into Iceland's glacial and volcanic landscape. Lunch is at your guide's favourite local restaurant on the South Coast, followed by Reynisfjara Black Sand Beach, with its basalt columns and powerful waves. Other South Coast sights are added if time allows.

Back at Base Camp, the LAVA Centre, an exhibition on volcanoes and earthquakes, is a 10-minute walk away if you'd like to visit on your own. Dinner is at Midgard Restaurant.

Day 4 · Saturday 10 October
Þórsmörk by Super Jeep

Today you travel into Þórsmörk Mountain Reserve in Midgard's modified 4×4 Super Jeeps, crossing glacial rivers that a normal car can't. Icelanders call it their "happy place": volcanic landscapes, three glaciers, river crossings, and short hikes to hidden spots along the way. Lunch is a packed lunchbox from Midgard Restaurant, out in the wilderness.

In the late afternoon you visit an Icelandic home, a local experience only Midgard offers. The local swimming pool is a 7-minute walk from Base Camp if you'd like a soak before dinner. Tonight is traditional Icelandic lamb, with vegetarian options available.

Day 5 · Sunday 11 October
Glacier Walk, Waterfalls & Aurora Igloo

Check out of Midgard Base Camp after breakfast for your final day with Midgard. You'll go on a guided ice walk on Sólheimajökull Glacier among ice formations, crevasses and blue ice. No experience is needed and your guide gives a safety briefing first. Afterwards you visit the glacier lagoon at its base and three waterfalls: Skógafoss, Seljalandsfoss (you can walk behind the falls when your guide judges it safe) and the hidden Gljúfrabúi.

Midgard drops you at Aurora Igloo South in Hella in the late afternoon. Check-in is self-service from 4:00pm: Igloo 14, key box by the front door, code 7417.

Day 6 · Monday 12 October
Private "Not in Your Guidebook" Tour

Check out of the igloo before 11:00am. Today is a private 6 to 7 hour tour with Midgard, just the two of you, to the places the guides go on their days off. Most of them are not mentioned in any guidebook, and if you come across anyone, it's most likely a local or a farmer.

The area is in and around Fljótshlíð, the "hill by the river", where many of the Icelandic Sagas took place. Places you may explore include the small canyons beneath Mt. Þórólfsfell, a 120m climb up Stóri Dímon for the view, the rivers, hidden waterfalls and caves around Mt. Þríhyrningur, and a secret spot in one of Iceland's few forests, where you can take a sip from an old well by a farm whose farmers lived to 100. Lunch is a burger stop along the way.

In the evening you return to Sand Hotel for two nights.

Day 7 · Tuesday 13 October
Free Day in Reykjavík

After breakfast at Sand Hotel, enjoy a full free day in the capital to explore at your own pace. Suggestions include Hallgrímskirkja church and its observation tower, Harpa Concert Hall on the harbour, a walk around the Old Harbour, and the Reykjavík Maritime Museum.

Also nearby: the Sun Voyager sculpture on the waterfront, a few minutes from Harpa; Perlan's observation deck; the Settlement Exhibition; the National Museum; and the Árbær Open Air Museum, with its turf houses.

Day 9 · Thursday 15 October
Canyon Baths & Húsafell at Your Own Pace

Your Húsafell Canyon Baths tour is at 10:00am, meeting at the Húsafell Activity Centre / Bistró car park. The 1 hour 45 minute guided tour starts with a short ride to the trailhead, then a relaxed walk past the two-tiered Langifoss waterfall and across a stream of glacial meltwater. It ends with an hour's soak in two hot-spring pools (30 to 41°C) built from local stone, looking out over the canyon, mountains and glaciers.

Apart from the Canyon Baths, today is yours to explore at your own pace. Hiking trails from the hotel range from 45 minutes to 10 hours, and a 3.5-hour trail reaches the Hraunfossar and Barnafoss waterfalls. The front desk can recommend one based on the weather. Lindin, the geothermally heated pool complex just outside the hotel, is open daily from 10:00am to 8:00pm, with two pools, two hot tubs and an ice-cold bath. Please note it is open to the public, not only hotel guests.
