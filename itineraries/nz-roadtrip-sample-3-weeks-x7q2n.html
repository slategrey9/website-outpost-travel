Welcome to Aotearoa

Land of the long white cloud

Aotearoa/New Zealand, where nature displays its full dramatic range. From vibrant cities steeped in Māori culture and culinary innovation to ancient glaciers and mountains, geothermal valleys steaming with otherworldly energy, and fiords carved by time itself, this is a destination that rewards curiosity at every turn.

Whether you're seeking adrenaline-fuelled adventures, tranquil wilderness experiences or the chance to explore Middle-earth brought to life, together with our local partner who specialises in self-drive tours, we bring an intimate knowledge to craft experiences that connect you with the real New Zealand, ensuring you return home with memories that transcend the ordinary.

The Journey

21 days across
both islands

This itinerary moves you through the full sweep of Aotearoa — beginning in Auckland's harbour-side city, threading through the geothermal heart of Rotorua and Taupō, and crossing to the South Island for Abel Tasman, Marlborough wine country, the rugged coast at Kaikōura, and the alpine drama of Tekapo, Mount Cook and Wanaka before closing in adventure-capital Queenstown.

It is a self-drive trip designed for a group of eight, with two premium 4WDs, three carefully placed flight legs, and a mix of luxury lodges, lakeside villas and full-villa exclusive-use stays.

Into the Shire

9:30am
Depart Auckland.
Auckland to Hobbiton: 2h 15m / 180km
Travel south on the Waikato expressway over the Bombay Hills, past lush farmlands and the winding Waikato River through the heart of dairy country. An optional brief stop in Hamilton (~30 mins detour) for the Hamilton Gardens.
12:00pm
Lunch at the Green Dragon Inn or Shire's Rest Cafe onsite.

Hobbiton Movie Set Signature Tour. A 2.5-hour guided walking tour through the Shire on the Alexander family sheep farm, where Sir Peter Jackson filmed The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit. 5:15pm
Redwoods Treewalk: a 30-minute self-paced walk on a 700m suspended walkway through Rotorua's iconic redwood forest. The walkway sits 20 metres above the forest floor at its peak, traversing 28 bridges between 27 majestic 120-year-old redwoods.
6:30pm
Check in at On The Point. A classic luxury lodge at the end of Kawaha Point with sweeping views across Lake Rotorua, antique-filled lounges and a timber-panelled dining room.
7:30pm
Dinner at the lodge or in Rotorua township.
Driving today 3h 30m / 256km (Auckland to Rotorua via Hobbiton)

Day 5

Taupō (Tongariro Day Trip)

The Volcanic Plateau

The Point Villas  ·  Night 2 of 2  ·  Villa with Breakfast Provisions

8:00am
Breakfast at the villa.
9:00am
Depart for Whakapapa Village at the foot of Tongariro National Park.
Taupō to Whakapapa: 1h 40m / 110km
Drive south along Lake Taupō's eastern shore then west on State Highway 47 through the Tongariro volcanic plateau. The three peaks of Tongariro, Ngauruhoe and Ruapehu rise dramatically from the desert-like landscape; Ngauruhoe was Mount Doom in The Lord of the Rings.
10:45am
Arrive Whakapapa Visitor Centre. Pick up a trail map.
11:00am
Begin the Taranaki Falls walk: a 2-hour, 6km loop starting 100m below the visitor centre. The lower track passes through tussock and beech forest with views of Ngauruhoe's symmetrical cone, descending to Wairere Stream and Cascade Falls before reaching Taranaki Falls itself, tumbling 20 metres over a 15,000-year-old andesite lava flow.
1:00pm
Walk ends. Late lunch at Whakapapa Village or pack a picnic.
2:30pm
Depart Whakapapa for Taupō.
4:15pm
Arrive back in Taupō. Late afternoon at leisure: Huka Falls if not visited en route, lakefront walk, or rest at the villa.
7:00pm
Dinner at the villa or in Taupō.
Check the NIWA Tongariro forecast before setting out; pack waterproofs, layers, sturdy boots and plenty of water. Alpine weather can change quickly even in October.
Driving today 3h 20m / 220km (Taupō to Whakapapa return)
