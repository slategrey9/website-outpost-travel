Welcome to Aotearoa

Land of the long white cloud

Aotearoa/New Zealand, where nature displays its full dramatic range. From vibrant cities steeped in Māori culture and culinary innovation to ancient glaciers and mountains, geothermal valleys steaming with otherworldly energy, and fiords carved by time itself, this is a destination that rewards curiosity at every turn.

Whether you're seeking adrenaline-fuelled adventures, tranquil wilderness experiences or the chance to explore Middle-earth brought to life, together with our local partner who specialises in self-drive tours, we bring an intimate knowledge to craft experiences that connect you with the real New Zealand, ensuring you return home with memories that transcend the ordinary.

The Journey

21 days across
both islands

This itinerary moves you through the full sweep of Aotearoa — beginning in Auckland's harbour-side city, threading through the geothermal heart of Rotorua and Taupō, and crossing to the South Island for Abel Tasman, Marlborough wine country, the rugged coast at Kaikōura, and the alpine drama of Tekapo, Mount Cook and Wanaka before closing in adventure-capital Queenstown.

It is a self-drive trip designed for a group of eight, with two premium 4WDs, three carefully placed flight legs, and a mix of luxury lodges, lakeside villas and full-villa exclusive-use stays.

Auckland  ·  Wed, 14 Oct 2026

Arrive in New Zealand

QT Auckland  ·  1 Night  ·  Bed & Breakfast

Nau mai, haere mai. Arrive in Auckland at 1:20pm aboard Singapore Airlines flight SQ285. A private Mercedes Sprinter will meet the group at the international terminal and transfer you into the city.

Spend the afternoon at leisure in Auckland, the City of Sails. Stroll the northern side of Viaduct Harbour with its thirty-plus cafés and bars, walk through to the Wynyard Quarter and North Wharf, or take a gentle ferry across to Devonport. In the other direction, Britomart's designer shops and laneway eateries are just a short walk along the waterfront. If golf is on the cards, Flush Golf in Britomart pairs high-definition simulators with a lounge-style bar.

QT Auckland sits in the heart of Viaduct Harbour with a Mediterranean-inspired rooftop bar. Rooms are equipped with Chromecast, Nespresso, wireless Bose speakers and Dyson Supersonic hairdryers.

Days 2–3

Rotorua  ·  Thu–Fri, 15–16 Oct 2026

On The Point

2 Nights  ·  B&B  ·  Lake View Lodge × 4

Collect two Toyota Prado 4WDs from Hertz at 9:00am and head south over the Bombay Hills, past lush Waikato farmland to Matamata. Spend the late morning on the Hobbiton Movie Set Signature Tour — a guided walk through the Shire past Bag End, Bagshot Row, the Mill and across the double-arch bridge to the Green Dragon Inn (approx. 2.5 hours).

Continue to Rotorua via the Fitzgerald Glade, with steam rising from the geothermal vents as you approach. A late-afternoon stop at Redwoods Treewalk takes you 20 metres above the forest floor on a 700m suspension walkway through 28 bridges and 27 redwood trees.

Day 3 is for Rotorua's Māori heritage and geothermal traditions. The Restorative Journey at Wai Ariki Hot Springs & Spa is a self-guided seven-step wellness ritual drawing on Ngāti Whakaue practices — waterfall showers, fire-and-ice rooms, mineral pools, mud lounge and steam cave. In the evening, the Mitai Māori Cultural Experience welcomes you for a hāngī dinner, traditional waka paddle, glow-worm walk and concert featuring the haka.

On The Point is a classic luxury lodge at the end of Kawaha Point, a refurbished two-storey manor with sweeping views across Lake Rotorua. Communal lounge, timber-panelled dining room and elegant billiard room pair antiques and art with modern interior design.

Days 4–5

Taupō  ·  Sat–Sun, 17–18 Oct 2026

The Point Villas

2 Nights  ·  Villa Only with Breakfast Provisions

A short, scenic drive south on State Highway 5 (1 hour / 82 km), passing Waimangu Volcanic Valley, the Lady Knox Geyser at Wai-O-Tapu, Wairakei Park, the Craters of the Moon and Huka Falls before arriving in Taupō. The afternoon is at leisure on the shores of New Zealand's largest lake.

On Day 5, drive south to Whakapapa Village (1h 40m / 110 km) at the foot of Tongariro National Park for the Taranaki Falls self-guided walk — a 2-hour, 6km loop starting 100m below the Whakapapa Visitor Centre. The lower track passes through tussock and beech forest with views of Ngauruhoe's symmetrical cone, descending to Wairere Stream and Cascade Falls before reaching Taranaki Falls itself, tumbling 20 metres over a 15,000-year-old andesite lava flow. Check the NIWA forecast before setting out and pack waterproofs, layers, sturdy boots and plenty of water — alpine weather can change quickly.

The Point Villas occupy a private estate on Whakamoenga Point with two villas across two and three bedrooms — master bedrooms open to glassed bathrooms for outdoor bathing, and the estate includes floodlit tennis, croquet, petanque and kayaks.

Days 6–8

Nelson  ·  Mon–Wed, 19–21 Oct 2026

Pīhopa Retreat

3 Nights  ·  B&B  ·  Four King Suites

Day 6 is a transit day across to the South Island. Drop the first rental at Taupō Airport and fly via Auckland to Nelson, arriving early afternoon. Collect the second pair of Toyota Prado 4WDs at Nelson Airport for the remainder of the trip.

On Day 7, embark in Stephens Bay for the Shared Best Abel Tasman Day Trip — a catamaran along the Abel Tasman National Park coastline, past Split Apple Rock, Kaiteriteri, Marahau and golden sandy bays out to the Tonga Island seal colony. The skipper adapts the route for dolphins, fur seals or a particular bay, and a sheltered lunch stop offers swimming, sea kayaking and short bush walks.

Day 8 is at leisure in Nelson, known for year-round sunshine, golden beaches, boutique wineries and a strong creative community. Options include cycling Rabbit Island via the "Flat-Bottomed Fairy" ferry, hiking from Flora Car Park into Kahurangi National Park (1.5–2 hours through subalpine forest with 360° views), or sampling artisan produce on the Great Taste Trail.

Pīhopa Retreat sits on 5.5 acres of park-like gardens — six premium suites with luxurious bedrooms and lounge areas, on the site of the original Nelson Diocese estate, an excellent base for the surrounding national parks, vineyards and beaches.

Day 9

Blenheim  ·  Thu, 22 Oct 2026

The Marlborough

1 Night  ·  B&B  ·  Premium Room × 4

Drive Nelson to Blenheim on State Highway 6 via Havelock (1h 45m / 123km). A film-buff detour at Pelorus Bridge Scenic Reserve — the river-barrel scene from The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug was filmed here, with a short bush walk through beech forest down to the riverbank.

Havelock, the Greenshell Mussel Capital, is worth a stop for fresh local mussels at Mills Bay Mussels, or coffee at the Tui Coffee cart by the marina.

Continue to Blenheim, the heart of Marlborough wine country. The Wairau Plains to the northwest hold some of New Zealand's finest wineries — Cloudy Bay, Allan Scott, Shingle Peak, Matua. The Marlborough Lodge is a grand country estate set in 6.5 hectares of park-like grounds and Sauvignon Blanc vineyards, with ten suites over two levels, an onsite chef team and pre-dinner canapés served from the Chapel Bar or by the outdoor fireplace.

Day 10

Kaikōura  ·  Fri, 23 Oct 2026

Manakau Lodge

1 Night  ·  Exclusive Use, Four Bedrooms

Blenheim to Kaikōura on State Highway 1 (1h 40m / 130km), heading south through the Awatere Valley wine region and the small towns of Seddon and Ward before the road begins to hug the rugged coastline with the Kaikōura Ranges to the right and the Pacific to the left. About an hour south of Blenheim, "The Store" at Kekerengu is a popular café and gift stop.

Arrive in Kaikōura for a 1:15pm Whale Watch Tour aboard Ocean's Cabin — New Zealand's only vessel-based whale-watching company, with year-round resident giant sperm whales. A typical tour also encounters fur seals, dusky dolphin pods and wandering albatross, with possible sightings of humpbacks, pilot whales, blue whales, southern right whales and orca.

Manakau Lodge offers exclusive use of a peaceful four-bedroom rural property — the entire property is yours for the night. World-class marine excursions and seafood restaurants are nearby, but the lodge itself is a place to relax in country surroundings.

Days 11–12

Christchurch  ·  Sat–Sun, 24–25 Oct 2026

The George

2 Nights  ·  B&B  ·  Junior Suite × 4

Kaikōura to Christchurch on SH1 (2h 25m / 180km), passing restored railway tunnels and cliff-hugging bends from the post-2016 earthquake rebuild. Stop at Cheviot to stretch the legs, with a 10-minute detour to Gore Bay for clay cliffs and quiet beach walks.

Through the Waipara Valley, one of New Zealand's premier pinot noir and riesling regions: Black Estate is open Thursday to Monday, 11:30am–4pm, reservation recommended. Waipara Springs Winery and Restaurant is open daily.

Day 12 is at leisure in the Garden City. Explore by double-decker bus, vintage bicycle, gondola, tram or Edwardian punt; visit the award-winning Christchurch Botanic Gardens, the Arts Centre and Christchurch Art Gallery; or take a day trip to Hanmer Springs (thermal pools), Akaroa (former French settlement on the Banks Peninsula), or back to Waipara for the wineries.

The George holds 53 spacious rooms with a one-to-one staff-to-guest ratio, sitting on Hagley Park and the river Avon, steps from the city centre and the cultural precinct.

Days 13–14

Lake Tekapo & Aoraki / Mount Cook  ·  Mon–Tue, 26–27 Oct 2026

Peppers Bluewater Lake Tekapo

2 Nights  ·  B&B  ·  Three-Bedroom Lake View Villa + Deluxe Lakeview Hotel Room

Christchurch to Tekapo on State Highway 1 (3h 10m / 230km), south across the Canterbury Plains and inland at Geraldine — known for gardens, orchards and the Geraldine Vintage Car Museum. Continue to Fairlie (don't miss the Fairlie Bakehouse pies), then over the Mackenzie Basin's golden plains and snow-capped peaks until Lake Tekapo's turquoise water comes into view. Stand at the Church of the Good Shepherd and walk up to the Mt John Observatory above the village for star-clear skies.

Optional that evening: the Summit Stargazing Experience with Dark Sky Project (1h 45m, NZ$225 per person). Board the tour bus to the summit of Mt John, one of the most southerly permanent optical observatories in the world, and use 16-inch, 11-inch and 9.25-inch telescopes guided by an astronomer. Includes return transport.

Day 14 is at leisure in Aoraki / Mount Cook National Park. Recommended: the Glacier Explorers tour by boat to the face of the Tasman Glacier; scenic helicopter or ski-plane flights over the icefields with two glacier landings; or the Hooker Valley walk (3–4 hours return) over swing bridges to the glacier lake with views of Aoraki and Hooker Glacier.

Peppers Bluewater rooms reflect the lake's turquoise hue with timber and leather textures, set against the foothills of the Southern Alps.

Days 15–16

Wanaka  ·  Wed–Thu, 28–29 Oct 2026

Marina Terrace Apartments

2 Nights  ·  Two-Bedroom Apartment × 2

Tekapo to Wanaka via the Mackenzie Country (2h 25m / 194km), passing Lake Pukaki's turquoise waters and a possible glimpse of Aoraki on a clear day. Stops at Twizel for coffee, Omarama for big skies (and optional hot pools), the Lindis Pass climb, and Tarras at the bottom for boutique shops and a cafe break.

An alternative extended route via the Crown Range — New Zealand's highest main road — adds about 90 minutes via Arrowtown (heritage gold-rush village, autumn colours) and Cardrona (the iconic Cardrona Hotel for lunch). Note the Crown Range can close in winter weather.

Day 16 begins with the 3.5-hour Lake, Cruise and Island Nature Walk with Eco Wanaka Adventures. A 35-minute boat cruise reaches Mou Waho Island Nature Reserve — a glacial-remnant island with a track climbing through native bush past the small Arethusa Pool to a 360° summit view. Tea and biscuits provided.

For an adrenaline addition, Skydive Wanaka offers an optional tandem 15,000ft jump (NZ$549 per person) — freefall at 200kph with unrivalled views of the lakes and Southern Alps.

Marina Terrace Apartments offer a premium lakefront stay with breathtaking views across Lake Wanaka and the Cardrona Valley.

Days 17–21

Queenstown  ·  Fri 30 Oct – Tue 3 Nov 2026

MajorDomo · Matakauri House

4 Nights  ·  Private Villa for 8

Wanaka to Queenstown via the Crown Range (1h 15m / 70km) — New Zealand's highest main road, climbing through alpine scenery with sweeping views over the Wakatipu Basin. Pause at Cardrona for the historic 1863 hotel, then detour into Arrowtown for tree-lined streets and gold-mining heritage. (Crown Range may close in winter; alternative is SH6 via Cromwell.)

Day 18 — Milford Fly/Cruise/Fly with Air Milford. A 35-minute scenic flight from Queenstown over alpine glaciers, rivers, lakes and rainforest to Milford Sound, then a Southern Discoveries cruise the full length of the fiord to the Tasman Sea (excellent wildlife viewing — seals, dolphins, penguins in season; picnic lunch and tea/coffee included), returning by coach to Milford Airport for the scenic flight back. 4.5 hours total.

Day 19 — Shared Wilderness Jet Boat with Dart River Adventures, departing Glenorchy. The original Wilderness Jet adventure on the braided, glacier-fed Dart River takes you deep into Mount Aspiring National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage area, with eco-tour guides bringing the scenery and Māori legends to life. 2 hours.

Day 20 — at leisure in Queenstown. Recommended walks: Sunshine Bay Track (4km return, 2.5 mi) for views to Cecil and Walter Peaks, Queenstown Hill Loop Track (5km return, 3.2 mi) up to the 'Basket of Dreams' lookout, or the longer Mount Crichton Loop Track (8km return, 5 mi) past waterfalls and gold-mining history. In the evening, optional Onsen Hot Pools (NZ$175 per pool, two bathers) — exclusive private cliffside pools high above the Shotover River Canyon, with shuttle service from town.

Day 21 — return the rental cars at Queenstown Airport. Air NZ NZ612 to Auckland (10:35am → 12:25pm), then between terminals (free 15-minute shuttle from Door 2 / Bus Stop C, or a 10–15 minute walk along the marked green line). Depart Auckland on Singapore Airlines SQ288 at 5:00pm. Haere rā.

Matakauri House sits in the Aspen Grove neighbourhood above Lake Wakatipu — floor-to-ceiling windows framing the Remarkables, two lounge and dining areas with open fireplaces, a walled garden with schist outdoor fireplace and BBQ, and a year-round heated plunge pool.
