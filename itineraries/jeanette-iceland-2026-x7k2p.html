Two itineraries, one extraordinary country

Both options span 11 nights in Iceland during October — prime Northern Lights season. The difference is in how you experience it: the classic highlights with a trusted operator, or a deeper, more private immersion with a boutique adventure base.

Option A

The Classic Route

Nordic Visitor Small Group + Extensions

11 nights 8 – 19 Oct Max 16 pax

A curated small-group tour covering Iceland's most iconic landscapes — Snæfellsnes Peninsula, Golden Circle, South Coast waterfalls, Glacier Lagoon and Diamond Beach — with independent extensions at Hotel Husafell and Reykjavík.

Snæfellsnes Peninsula Diamond Beach Glacier Lagoon Vatnajökull Ice Cave Þórsmörk Day Trip
~SGD 6,500 per person ~SGD 13,000 for 2
Option B

The Immersive Retreat

Midgard Adventure + Extensions

11 nights 7 – 18 Oct Private & small group

A more intimate experience built around Midgard Base Camp in South Iceland — with structured Northern Lights viewing every evening, a Super Jeep Þórsmörk adventure, and a private "Not in Your Guidebook" tour through hidden canyons and secret waterfalls.

Northern Lights nightly Super Jeep Þórsmörk Private Hidden Iceland Katla Ice Cave 3 nights at Husafell
~SGD 6,600 per person ~SGD 13,200 for 2
