The Journey

13 days across
East Africa

This itinerary brings together two of the most extraordinary wildlife experiences on the planet: mountain gorilla trekking in the ancient Bwindi Impenetrable Forest, and the Great Migration in the northern Serengeti at peak river-crossing season.

The journey moves from Uganda into Tanzania — from the quiet intimacy of the forest to the vast, open drama of the plains — before closing with two nights on the Zanzibar coast. All safari camps are operated by Asilia Africa, with private transfers and internal flights throughout.

Thursday, 1 July  ·  Entebbe, Uganda

Arrival in Entebbe

No. 5 Boutique Hotel  ·  1 Night  ·  Bed & Breakfast

Your journey begins with an arrival night in Entebbe on the northern shore of Lake Victoria. A private transfer from the airport brings you to No. 5 Boutique Hotel, where you settle in before an early departure to Bwindi the following morning.

Private transfer from Entebbe International Airport (EBB)
Early departure the next morning — 06:00 transfer to airport
02–04

Friday–Sunday, 2–4 July  ·  Bwindi, Uganda

Asilia Erebero Hills

3 Nights  ·  Full Board

A short flight from Entebbe brings you into Kihihi, at the edge of the Bwindi Impenetrable Forest. Erebero Hills is Asilia's newest Uganda property, set deep in mountain gorilla country. Your trekking permit is included for Day 3 — and the option to go again on Day 4 is available at an additional cost.

Aerolink Uganda: Entebbe (EBB) → Kihihi, 07:00 (shared light aircraft)
Gorilla trek — Day 3, Saturday 3 July (1 permit per person included)
Batwa Community Experience and nature walk
1 × 30-minute massage per person included
Optional second gorilla trek on Day 4 (USD 800 per person)
05–07

Monday–Wednesday, 5–7 July  ·  Northern Serengeti, Tanzania

Olakira Migration Camp

3 Nights  ·  Full Board

A cross-border hop — back through Entebbe and on to Kogatende in the northern Serengeti — brings you into Tanzania at peak migration season. Olakira follows the herds each year, and in July that means positioning close to the Mara River as the wildebeest begin to build for the crossings.

Aerolink: Kihihi → Entebbe (08:35), then Auric Air: Entebbe → Kogatende (12:15)
Scheduled open vehicle game drives, mornings and evenings
Mara River access — July is peak river-crossing season
All Serengeti park and camping fees included
08–10

Thursday–Saturday, 8–10 July  ·  Ngorongoro, Tanzania

The Highlands

3 Nights  ·  Full Board

A morning flight south brings you to the Ngorongoro Highlands, where The Highlands sits on the conservation area rim overlooking the caldera. Game drives, volcanic crater hikes, and the option of a descent into the Ngorongoro Crater itself make for a layered final act before the coast.

Auric Air: Kogatende → Lake Manyara (LKY), 10:20 (shared light aircraft)
Scheduled game drives and Olmoti and Empakaai Crater hikes included
Optional: Ngorongoro Crater descent (USD 70 pp) and Maasai cultural visit (USD 30 pp)
All Ngorongoro park and concession fees included
11–13

Sunday–Tuesday, 11–13 July  ·  Zanzibar

Zanzibar Palace Hotel

2 Nights  ·  Bed & Breakfast

A midday flight delivers you to Zanzibar by mid-afternoon. Two nights at Zanzibar Palace Hotel in Stone Town offer a natural wind-down after the intensity of the safari: the spice-scented alleys of the old town, the coast, the slower rhythm of the Swahili shoreline. A private transfer brings you to the airport on Day 13.

Auric Air: Lake Manyara → Zanzibar (ZNZ), 12:00 (shared light aircraft)
Private road transfer on arrival and departure (Zantours)
Day 13: Transfer to Abeid Amani Karume International Airport (ZNZ)
