Day by Day

A private Mandarin-speaking driver-guide travels with you through the South Island, and a second guide looks after your days in Auckland, so there is nothing to arrange along the way. The South Island is some of the most beautiful country on earth, and this route is shaped to take in its greatest sights: the lakes and mountains of Queenstown, the glaciers and dark skies of Aoraki/Mount Cook, and the garden city of Christchurch, with the drives between them counted among the world's most scenic. Several days are left open for rest along the way.

Day 1
Monday, 14 December
Private villa, Queenstown Hill
Three-bedroom villa
BLD
Arrive in Auckland and fly south to Queenstown
  • Private airport transfer. On arrival at Auckland International Airport, you will be met by your private driver and vehicle and transferred to the Domestic Terminal prior to your onward flight. (International flights are your own arrangement.)
  • Domestic flight to Queenstown. One of the most beautiful arrivals anywhere, the plane dropping in between the mountains to land beside Lake Wakatipu.
  • Settle in on Queenstown Hill. Your Mandarin-speaking driver-guide meets you and takes you up to the villa, a three-bedroom home with lake and mountain views and the town centre a ten-minute walk downhill. The evening is yours.
Day 2
Tuesday, 15 December
Private villa, Queenstown Hill
Three-bedroom villa
BLD
Glenorchy: peaks, a glacier and a jet boat
  • Private Peaks to Paradise Flight & Jet Boat. From 8:45am, a morning that is hard to better. You lift off by helicopter from Glenorchy over Lake Whakatipu and the Dart River, setting down high on exclusive Mount Alfred where the engine falls silent for 360-degree views, then again beneath the hanging glacier and waterfalls of the Earnslaw Burn. From Paradise, a wilderness jet boat carries you back down the braided river through the Te Wāhipounamu World Heritage wilderness, with a few spins along the way. (Weather dependent.)
  • Afternoon in Glenorchy. After the jet boat, a relaxed afternoon among the lakes, native forest and Lord of the Rings filming locations, with a generous platter lunch at Mrs Woolly's (settled locally). Back to Queenstown in the late afternoon.
Day 3
Wednesday, 16 December
Private villa, Queenstown Hill
Three-bedroom villa
BLD
Central Otago wine & Cardrona Distillery
  • Gibbston Valley Wine Tour. Into the Gibbston Valley, the "Valley of the Vines" set between schist mountains and the Kawarau gorge, for two tastings at some of Central Otago's finest pinot noir cellar doors, with a relaxed winery lunch along the way (settled locally).
  • Cardrona Distillery. In the afternoon, on to the remote Cardrona Distillery for a 75-minute tour, watching whisky and gin made from scratch and tasting across the range. Back to the villa by evening.
Day 4
Thursday, 17 December
Mt Cook Lakeside Retreat
Two-bedroom villa · private Fantail Spa
BLD
To Mount Cook, on one of the world's most scenic drives
  • Drive to Mount Cook with touring. This is no ordinary road trip. Take in the breathtaking scenery as you head north with your guide, up over the Lindis Pass and into the Mackenzie Country, the land opening into golden tussock grass and turquoise glacial lakes.
  • Mt Cook Lakeside Retreat. Arrive mid-afternoon at your two-bedroom villa on the shore of Lake Pūkaki, with Aoraki/Mount Cook filling the view. A bottle of New Zealand sparkling is waiting, and dinner is a three-course evening meal, served either at the retreat's restaurant, The Moraine, or brought to your villa for a private dinner.
  • Dark Sky Reserve Stargazing Experience. Later in the evening, join the team in the Pūkaki Cellar Observatory, in the heart of the Aoraki Mackenzie Dark Sky Reserve and among the clearest night skies on earth. A glass of wine or whisky in the cellar, then an astronomer to guide you through the southern stars by telescope. (Weather dependent.)
Day 5
Friday, 18 December
Mt Cook Lakeside Retreat
Two-bedroom villa · private Fantail Spa
BLD
Glaciers by helicopter and ski plane
  • The Ultimate Alpine Experience. The day's highlight, and the finest way to see the Southern Alps: a helicopter and ski-plane flight across the Main Divide, over the Fox and Franz Josef Glaciers and along the edge of the Tasman Sea, before a snow landing high on the Tasman Glacier. Then back over the Grand Plateau beneath the east face of Aoraki.
  • Afternoon at Mount Cook. Rejoin your guide to explore the national park at your own pace: the Hooker Valley Track, the Sir Edmund Hillary Alpine Centre, or simply the shore of Lake Pūkaki with the mountain above.
  • Dinner at the retreat. Breakfast, a hiker's lunch and a three-course dinner are provided each day of your stay.
Day 6
Saturday, 19 December
Mt Cook Lakeside Retreat
Two-bedroom villa · private Fantail Spa
BLD
A day at the retreat
  • A day at leisure. Nothing planned, on the retreat's 70-hectare estate above Lake Pūkaki.
  • Around the estate. Guided estate walks with a picnic lunch, a forest-bathing trail through the glacial woodland, tennis in the long summer dusk, yoga, a ride along part of the Alps 2 Ocean trail, or a chef's tasting menu drawn from the Mackenzie. Or simply enjoy your private Fantail Spa, with its sauna, outdoor hot tub, workout room and massage room, open to the lake and mountain views in the privacy of your villa. Breakfast, lunch and dinner are provided at the retreat.
