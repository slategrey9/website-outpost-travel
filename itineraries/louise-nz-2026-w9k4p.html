Day by Day

Your own Mandarin-speaking driver-guide travels with you from Auckland through to Hawke's Bay, so there is nothing to arrange along the way. No single drive is more than about three hours, and two days are left completely free.

Day 1
Saturday, 12 December
Park Hyatt Auckland
2 × Harbour View Twin Room
BLD
Arrive in Auckland
  • Private airport transfer. Your Mandarin-speaking driver-guide meets you as you land at Auckland Airport and takes you the short drive to the Park Hyatt, right on the water at Wynyard Quarter (about 30 minutes).
  • Evening at leisure. Settle in and take the evening gently after your flight. When you're ready, there's a 25-metre infinity pool, a spa, and four restaurants and bars, with the harbour just outside.
Day 2
Sunday, 13 December
Park Hyatt Auckland
2 × Harbour View Twin Room
BLD
Auckland at leisure
  • Free day in Auckland. A whole day to yourselves, with nothing planned. Wake late and take it slowly.
  • Ideas, at your own arrangement. If you feel like venturing out: the wine and arts villages of Matakana to the north, the white-sand beaches of Tāwharanui, the Tiritiri Matangi bird sanctuary, or a ferry to Rangitoto, the volcanic island out in the harbour.
  • Optional distillery tours. Visits to Thomson Distillery and 1919 Distillery can be arranged if of interest.
Day 3
Monday, 14 December
Park Hyatt Auckland
2 × Harbour View Twin Room
BLD
Waiheke Island, food & wine
  • Ferry to Waiheke Island. A short morning sail across the Hauraki Gulf, around 40 minutes, with your guide meeting you at Matiatia Wharf for the day ahead.
  • Private Waiheke Food & Wine Tour. A relaxed day among the island's vineyards: tastings at two standout wineries, a long lunch at one of them, and plenty of local olive oil and produce to try. Between tastings there are wide views over the gulf, and time to wander into the rainforest and forage native leaves to take home for tea.
  • Return ferry to Auckland in the late afternoon.
Day 4
Tuesday, 15 December
Pullman Rotorua
2 × Lake View Twin Room
BLD
Auckland to Rotorua, via Hobbiton
  • Hobbiton Movie Set Signature Tour. Set off after breakfast for the drive south, close to three hours, to the Hobbiton Movie Set near Matamata. A guided two-hour walk leads you through the rolling farmland Sir Peter Jackson chose for the Shire: the hobbit holes tucked into the hillside, the round green door of Bag End, the double-arch bridge, and a drink to finish in the Green Dragon Inn.
  • On to Rotorua. From there it is a little over an hour on to the Pullman, beside the lake in Rotorua. The evening is yours.
Day 5
Wednesday, 16 December
Pullman Rotorua
2 × Lake View Twin Room
BLD
Rotorua by floatplane, and a Māori celebration
  • Mount Tarawera & Hot Pools by Floatplane. Today's highlight is a floatplane, usually flown in the morning. You lift off from the Rotorua lakefront, out over Mokoia Island and the crater of Mount Tarawera, and the steaming, colour-streaked valleys Māori call the "Sisters of Fire". You touch down at Orakei Korako, the "Hidden Valley", for an hour among the silica terraces and geysers, then fly on to a hot pool reached only by water, where 90 minutes slip quietly by.
  • Afternoon at leisure. You are usually back by around midday, leaving the afternoon free to rest at the hotel or wander down to the lake before the evening out.
  • Tū Te Rā Summer Harvest Celebration. From around 5pm, a summer feast in a forest Māori village: canapés to begin, stories told through haka and song, and a three-course hāngī drawn from the earth oven, all under the trees and firelight.
Day 6
Thursday, 17 December
Pullman Rotorua
2 × Lake View Twin Room
BLD
Forest canopy & a kiwi encounter
  • Exclusive Canopy Tour. Take to the treetops on an exclusive canopy tour: a few gentle hours of ziplines, swing bridges and platforms high in 500 hectares of native forest, birdsong all around.
  • National Kiwi Hatchery Tour. An hour in the company of New Zealand's most elusive bird, including the nocturnal house where you might just spot a kiwi. Every ticket supports the breeding programme.
  • The rest of the day is free, to enjoy Rotorua however you please.
Day 7
Friday, 18 December
Hilton Lake Taupō
2 × Guest Room, Twin
BLD
Rotorua to Taupō
  • Drive to Taupō. An easy hour's drive south, with your guide pausing for the views along the way.
  • Check in at Lake Taupō. The Hilton Lake Taupō looks out over New Zealand's largest lake, the Tongariro volcanoes rising across the water. The rest of the day is yours to unwind.
Day 8
Saturday, 19 December
Hilton Lake Taupō
2 × Guest Room, Twin
BLD
A day on Lake Taupō
  • Lake Cruise & Trout Fishing. Spend the morning out on the water, gliding into quiet coves you can only reach by boat, the volcanoes Ruapehu, Ngāuruhoe and Tongariro filling the southern skyline. Lunch and fishing licences are included, and there is every chance of a Taupō trout on the line.
  • Afternoon Geothermal Tour. In the afternoon, your guide takes you out to the geothermal parks and the roar of Huka Falls. (Park entry fees are payable locally.)
Day 9
Sunday, 20 December
The Manse, Hastings
Exclusive use · 2 suites, King
BLD
Taupō to Hawke's Bay, via Craggy Range
  • Drive to Hawke's Bay. Leave Taupō mid-morning and drive about two hours south into the wine country of Hawke's Bay, drawing up at the Craggy Range estate.
  • Giants Gourmet Experience at Craggy Range. Here you settle in for a long lunch, from late morning into the afternoon: a tour of the estate and cellars, a stroll through the kitchen gardens, and a six-course tasting menu, each course poured with the estate's own wines.
  • Check in at The Manse. Then a short drive on to The Manse, your lodge near Hastings, yours alone for three nights. Dinner is laid on your first evening, with local wine to welcome you.
Day 10
Monday, 21 December
The Manse, Hastings
Exclusive use · 2 suites, King
BLD
The best of Hawke's Bay
  • Private Full-Day Hawke's Bay Tour. A full day exploring Hawke's Bay with a local guide (your Mandarin-speaking driver-guide leaves this morning). The Art Deco streets of Napier, the sculpture gardens at Birdwoods, the long views from Te Mata Peak, a stop for honey at Arataki, and a last tasting at Craggy Range. Two wine tastings and a two-course lunch are included.
Day 11
Tuesday, 22 December
The Manse, Hastings
Exclusive use · 2 suites, King
BLD
Hawke's Bay at leisure
  • Free day in Hawke's Bay, at the lodge or out and about, however suits you.
  • Ideas, at your own arrangement. Perhaps the Art Deco streets of Napier, a trip out to the Cape Kidnappers gannet colony, the wineries around Havelock North, or the beaches at Waimarama and Ocean Beach.
Day 12
Wednesday, 23 December
Departure
Napier Airport
BLD
Fly home via Auckland
  • Transfer to Napier Airport. A short drive of about 25 minutes from the lodge.
  • Domestic flight to Auckland. From there, a flight of around 1¼ hours to Auckland, connecting with your journey home. (Domestic and international flights are not included.)
