New Year in Phu Quoc

Early January 2027 · Family of 8: 4 adults, 1 baby, 1 toddler, 10yo, 12yo

At a glance

I've selected three options for Phu Quoc for the group:

  1. New WorldYou are already familiar with this one, and it provides the best value.
  2. InterContinentalIf you would like a change of scenery. I also want to highlight that their three-bedroom villa has a fab layout and is almost the same price as three classic rooms, which is rare. So, if you're looking at the Intercon, the villa is worth considering. As the Intercon is also on the west coast of the island, you'll get lovely sunset views.
  3. RegentThe Regent came in higher than I thought it would, but I've left it in as an FYI.

Note that the two-bedroom classic room option for InterContinental has two twin beds (not doubles or queens), so it would be a bit of a squeeze for four people. The maximum occupancy is two adults and one child on existing bedding, so if 2A + 2C were to share a room we can enquire about the possibility of a rollaway bed.

Note that for the InterContinental there is a 3-bedroom residence layout, but unfortunately it is not available for your dates.

One more thing to note is that the dates are not identical. The Regent has a minimum 5 night stay, hence the date is as you see below.

Where they are

The airport sits in the middle of the island. New World is south at Bãi Khem, one of the quieter beaches on the island, a little under half an hour from the airport and walking distance from Sunset Town. The InterContinental and the Regent are neighbours on Bãi Trường, the long west coast beach that runs south of Duong Dong, which puts the sunset in front of you and the airport about a quarter of an hour behind.
