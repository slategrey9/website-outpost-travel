Where they are

The airport is in the north, which is why The Slate is ten minutes away and the InterContinental is forty. Everything on Phuket faces west into the Andaman. Koh Yao Yai sits behind the island in Phang Nga Bay, and that turns out to matter in October.

Nai Yang

The Slate

D-Buk Family Suite · 76 sqm

USD 804
Four nights, from

The room

Two ways to do it here, and the gap between them is smaller than you would expect.

  • D-Buk Family Suite76 sqm. A king with an oversized day bed alongside it, and room for a cot without the floor disappearing.
  • One-bedroom Pool VillaThe newest rooms on the property, with a pool of your own. The step up, and worth pricing given where the entry rate sits.

Why this one

Ten minutes from the airport. On four nights that is worth more than it sounds, and with a baby it is worth a great deal more. Bill Bensley built the place around Phuket's tin-mining history, so it has a point of view rather than the usual resort finish, and the national park behind Nai Yang keeps the beach quiet.

What is included

  • Tin Box Kids' Club
  • A family pool, separate from the two adult-only pools
  • Cot, high chair and steriliser, which I would arrange ahead as I did in Japan

Bang Tao

Banyan Tree Phuket

Banyan Pool Villa · 170 sqm, one bedroom

USD 1,976
Four nights, from

The room

Every room here is a villa, and every villa has its own walled pool. That is the whole argument for it.

  • Banyan Pool Villa170 sqm, one bedroom, private pool. The natural choice for the four of you.
  • Serenity Pool Villa140 sqm, the smaller one bedroom, with a 17 sqm pool and jet pool.

Why this one

A pool behind your own wall is a different holiday at this age. Naps happen when they need to, bedtime happens on time, and nobody is walking a damp four-year-old back across a resort at six in the evening. It is the reason I would put this above the InterContinental despite the price.

What is included

  • Terra Kids Club, nine until six daily
  • The Laguna Phuket complex, so the lagoon, the shuttle boats and a spread of restaurants without leaving
  • Twenty minutes from the airport

InterContinental Phuket Resort

The room

The only one of the four where a proper door between you and the children is straightforward.

  • 1 Bedroom Villa, private pool210 sqm with a 50 sqm pool, butler service and full Club InterContinental privileges.
  • Two connecting Classic RoomsThe conventional arrangement, and cheaper. Connecting rooms have to be requested and confirmed rather than assumed, so I would put that in writing at the time of booking.

Why this one

The most complete resort of the four. A long stretch of Kamala beachfront, several pools, and Planet Trekkers a few steps from the villas. Club InterContinental is the quiet advantage: breakfast, afternoon tea and an evening hour, all of which removes a run of small decisions on days when you have not slept.

What is included

  • Planet Trekkers kids' club

Getting there

Road · 40 minutes

The longest road transfer of the three on Phuket.

Worth knowing

Forty minutes each way is the trade for the scale of the place. On a longer stay I would not mention it. On four nights it is most of an afternoon.

Kamala faces west, so the sea in early October will be doing what the rest of the west coast is doing.

Koh Yao Yai · Phang Nga Bay

Anantara Koh Yao Yai

Family Suite · from 90 sqm

USD 1,160
Four nights, from

The room

Yes to villas, and they are the reason to look properly at this one.

  • Family SuiteFrom 90 sqm. The family suites are built for exactly this age, with bunk beds, slides and play areas set into the room.
  • Lagoon or Beachfront Pool VillaOne and two bedroom pool villas along the shoreline, if you would rather have the pool than the bunk beds.

Why this one

I think this is the one you had in mind. There is no Anantara on Koh Yao Noi. Theirs is on Koh Yao Yai, the larger island next door, and it looks out at the limestone karsts rather than the open sea. It has the best kids' club of the four by a distance, the largest in southern Thailand, and the water in front of it is sheltered when the Phuket beaches are not.

What is included

  • The kids' club, and family suites designed around it
  • A family pool alongside the main pool
  • Speedboat transfers on a published timetable

Getting there

Road 20 min · boat 35 min

By road to Laem Sai Pier, then the resort speedboat to its own jetty.

Worth knowing

The boat is the whole question. It is well run and it goes to a timetable, but it is two transfers in each direction with a baby, and in early October the bay can move about. On four nights, that is what you are weighing against the sheltered water and the kids' club.

Second cheapest of the four, which surprises people.

Early October is the tail of the green season. Warm water, bright mornings, and showers that build through the afternoon. The west coast beaches, which is where three of these sit, can run up to two metres of swell with red flags on the bigger days. It is why I have weighted this towards the pools and the kids' clubs rather than the sea, and it is also why the rates are the lowest they will be all year.
