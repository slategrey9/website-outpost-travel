At a glance

Following your note, I've kept all four you mentioned and set them side by side here. Three sit on Phuket itself. The fourth is out in Phang Nga Bay, and there's a small correction attached to it: there is no Anantara on Koh Yao Noi. Theirs is on Koh Yao Yai, the larger island next door, and it's the one I think you had in mind.

The stay is short and the children are small, so this is weighted towards how quickly you can be settled and what the resort carries once you are. Early October falls at the tail of the green season, which keeps rates at their lowest of the year and puts some swell on the west coast, so the pools and the kids' clubs count for more than the sea does.

Where they are

The airport sits in the north of the island, which is why The Slate is ten minutes away and the InterContinental is forty. Everything on Phuket faces west into the Andaman. Koh Yao Yai lies behind the island in Phang Nga Bay, sheltered by it, and in early October that tells.

The Slate

D-Buk Family Suite · 76 sqm

USD 804

The rooms

Two worth pricing here, and less between them than you might think.

  • D-Buk Family Suite76 square metres, a king with an oversized day bed alongside and space for a cot.
  • One-bedroom Pool VillaThe newest rooms on the property, with a pool of your own off the terrace.

The place

Ten minutes from the airport, on Nai Yang beach with national park behind it, so the sand stays quiet. Bill Bensley built the resort around Phuket's old tin-mining history and it wears that properly, in black steel and salvaged timber, with a good deal more character than a beach hotel usually carries.

What's included

Banyan Pool Villa · 170 sqm, one bedroom

USD 1,976

The rooms

Every room here is a villa, and every villa has a walled garden with its own pool.

  • Banyan Pool Villa170 square metres, one bedroom, private pool and outdoor sala.
  • Serenity Pool Villa140 square metres, the smaller one bedroom, with a 17 square metre pool and jet pool.

The place

Twenty minutes from the airport at Bang Tao, inside the Laguna complex, where a lagoon links several resorts and a shuttle boat runs between them. A pool behind your own wall changes the shape of a day when the children are this small. Naps happen when they need to, and the evening doesn't depend on walking anyone back across a resort.

What's included

Getting there

Road 20 min · boat 35 min

By road to Laem Sai Pier, then the resort speedboat across to its own jetty.

Worth knowing

The boat is the thing to weigh. It runs to a timetable and it's well handled, but it's two transfers in each direction, and the bay can move about in early October. Against that you have the calmest water of the four and the best-equipped kids' club.

Choosing Between Them

They pull in different directions rather than ranking cleanly, so here is the shortest way to think about it.

If the short transfer matters
The SlateTen minutes from the terminal, so you can be in the water by early afternoon on the first day and out of the door easily on the last. The rate also leaves room to move up from the family suite into the pool villa.
If you want a pool of your own
Banyan Tree PhuketA walled garden and a private pool at every villa, twenty minutes from the airport, with the lagoon and the restaurants of Laguna to wander into. One bedroom, so you're all in together.
If you'd rather everything were on site
InterContinental PhuketThe fullest resort of the four, with the Club lounge running through the day and the kids' club close by, and the one place where a second room with a door is simple to arrange. Forty minutes each way is the trade.
If you'd trade a boat for calm water
Anantara Koh Yao YaiSheltered swimming through a season when the west coast is rough, and the best-equipped kids' club here. It asks a road transfer and a speedboat of you in each direction.
