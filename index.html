Good to Know

Common questions

Is there a fee?

For hotel bookings, no. The hotels pay us a commission and you still get the Virtuoso perks. For end-to-end trip planning, we access trade rates through a trusted network of ground suppliers; our margin is built into the trip cost, and overall pricing stays competitive.

What budget should I plan for?

Hotels from around USD 300 a night in Southeast Asia, and from USD 500 in most other places. End-to-end journeys start from USD 500 per person per day, excluding flights. We'll always be straightforward about what's achievable.

What happens on a discovery call?

A short, no-obligation chat, usually 15 to 20 minutes, to understand your trip and whether we're the right fit. No pressure. No commitment.

How do we get started?

Fill out the Start Planning form, message on WhatsApp, or email us. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll take it from there.
