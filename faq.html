How we work

Outpost Travel is a travel concierge based in Singapore, working with clients worldwide, and an independent affiliate of Alchemist Travel, a Virtuoso member agency. We handle hotel bookings with Virtuoso and preferred partner benefits, and end-to-end planning for more complex journeys. Whether it's a single hotel stay or a multi-week expedition, we take care of the details so you can focus on the trip itself.

A discovery call is a short, no-obligation conversation, usually 15 to 20 minutes, where we'll talk through your trip, your travel style, and what you're looking for. It's a chance for us to understand your needs, and for you to get a sense of whether we're the right fit.

No pressure, no commitment.

The easiest way is to fill in the Start Planning form or message us on WhatsApp. Just tell us a little about what you have in mind and when you're looking to travel, and we'll take it from there.

If you'd prefer email, we're at hello@outposttravel.co.

Pricing

For hotel bookings, there's no fee to you. Outpost Travel earns a commission directly from the hotels. Through Virtuoso and our preferred partnerships across the major luxury groups, we also unlock perks at no additional cost: room upgrades, hotel credits, daily breakfast, early check-in and late checkout.

For end-to-end trip planning, we work with a trusted network of ground suppliers covering accommodation, guides, transport, and experiences. Through these relationships, we access trade and net rates that aren't publicly available. Our margin is built into the trip cost, so you see one clear price, and overall pricing stays competitive.

Budgets vary by destination, seasonality, and travel style.

For hotel bookings, Outpost Travel typically works with properties from USD 500 per room per night onwards. In Southeast Asia, rates start from around USD 300 per room per night.

For end-to-end trip planning, journeys start from USD 500 per person per day, excluding flights. Each journey is bespoke, so costs are highly variable depending on destination, group size, and the experiences involved.

Virtuoso is a global network of luxury travel advisors. Outpost books through our host agency, Alchemist Travel, a Virtuoso member, so your stays carry Virtuoso benefits. Many of the world's best hotels, cruise lines and ground operators offer Virtuoso clients benefits they don't extend to anyone else.

At a Virtuoso hotel that typically means daily breakfast for two, a hotel credit (often USD 100) to use during your stay, an upgrade on arrival when available, and early check-in or late checkout on request. Benefits vary by property, and we confirm them in writing with every quote.

At most major hotel groups, yes. Bookings made through us usually still earn points and recognise your member status, just as if you'd booked direct. A few independent and luxury brands don't run points programmes at all. If points matter to you, tell us and we'll check before you book.
